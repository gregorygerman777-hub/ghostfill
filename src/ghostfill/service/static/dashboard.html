Engine

Paper mode. No real money. Every fill is simulated against live mainnet quotes with fees, slippage and random failures. Nothing is ever signed or sent. Most strategies lose money after costs, paper results overstate real ones (no MEV), and the token screen reduces but does not eliminate rug pull risk.
